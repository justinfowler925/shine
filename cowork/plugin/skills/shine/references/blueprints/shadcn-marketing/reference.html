How it works

  1. 1. The call is scored

    Answers are analysed as they are spoken; nothing is recorded for later review.

  2. 2. The risk lands on the claim

    The score, the questions that drove it and the source appear on the record, not in an inbox.

  3. 3. The adjuster decides

    One action, with a rationale, and the decision is written back to the system of record.
