## Regions (shared rules)

1. One composition job per page — not a dashboard of leftovers.
2. One filled primary in the fold.
3. Tables own the work object; fields group by section; nav shows active state.
4. Overlays (when needed) follow SDS Dialog max-width discipline — not full-bleed essays.
5. Never accordion landfill under a summary lead; never detached overflow.

## Do not

- Treat these as a second house kit. They teach silhouette; consumers stay on shadcn.
- Cite HeroUI/Material/Bootstrap as installable runtimes.
- Replace `shadcn-operate-decide` with a freestyle invent when denoise feels stuck.
